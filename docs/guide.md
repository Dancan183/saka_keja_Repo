# Saka Keja User Guide

For tenants, landlords and administrators.

## 1. Creating an account

You verify your phone and your email. Your account is created only after both.

1. Open **Register** and fill in your details. Choose **Tenant** (looking for a house) or **Landlord** (listing houses).
2. Enter your phone number and press **Confirm Phone Number**. A 6-digit code arrives by SMS. It is valid for **5 minutes**.
3. Enter the code and press **Verify Code**.
4. Submit the form. A 6-digit code is sent to your email. It is valid for **10 minutes**.
5. Enter it on the next page. Your account is now created.

Tips:

- Use the **same browser** from start to finish. Your unfinished sign-up is tied to it and is kept for 30 minutes.
- You can ask for a new SMS code after 60 seconds, and a new email code after 60 seconds.
- You get 5 tries per code. After that, request a new one.
- Limits: 5 SMS codes per phone number per hour.
- Check your spam folder for the email.

## 2. Logging in and out

Log in with your **username**, not your email. If you forget your password, use **Forgot password** and follow the link sent to your email.

## 3. Tenants

**Search.** On the home page or **Houses**, filter by location, house type, price range, bedrooms (exact number) and bathrooms (at least that many). Results show 12 per page.

**Contact a landlord.** Open a house and press **Contact the landlord** to send an inquiry. You must be logged in. The landlord's replies appear in **My inquiries**. You can also call or WhatsApp from the house page.

**Review a landlord.** After you have sent an inquiry to a landlord, a review option appears. You may leave one review per landlord, with stars and a comment. Reviews of landlords are public.

## 4. Landlords

**Add a house.** Open **Add house**, fill in the type, rent, bedrooms, bathrooms, location and description, then save.

**Add photos.** Open your house and choose **Upload images**. Use photos under about 4 MB each; larger ones may fail on the live site. Resize them first if needed.

**Manage houses.** **My houses** lists your listings. Edit or remove them there.

**Inbox.** Messages from tenants appear in **Inbox**. Open one to read and reply.

**Reviews.** After a tenant has contacted you, you can leave a private review about them. Only you and the site admins can see it. Reviews tenants leave about you are public.

## 5. Reviews: the rules

- Only after an inquiry between the two of you.
- One review per pair.
- You can report a review. A report does not hide it automatically. An admin decides.

## 6. Administrators

Sign in at `/admin/`.

- **Locations:** add or rename the areas shown in search.
- **Users:** change a user's role, deactivate an account.
- **Houses:** edit or remove listings that break the rules.
- **Reviews:** hide a review that is abusive or false (open it and untick **Is visible**). Check reported reviews regularly.

Do not delete a user who has houses, inquiries or reviews without checking: deleting may be blocked by linked records. Deactivate the account instead.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No SMS code | Wait 60 seconds, press resend. Check the number format (for example 07xx or +2547xx). Remember the 5-per-hour limit. |
| No email code | Check spam. Wait 60 seconds and resend. Confirm the email address is spelled correctly. |
| "Code expired" | Request a new code. |
| Too many wrong attempts | Request a new code. |
| Sign-up was lost | You probably switched browser or waited over 30 minutes. Register again. |
| Cannot log in | Use your username, not your email. Use Forgot password if needed. |
| Photo will not upload | Make it smaller than about 4 MB. |
| Cannot review someone | You need an inquiry between you first. |
