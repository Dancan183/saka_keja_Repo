Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Saka Keja collects, why, who sees it, and what rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Draft notice: this text describes how the site works today. Have a Kenyan lawyer review it, and check whether the business must register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), before you rely on it.

1. Who is responsible

Saka Keja decides how and why your information is used (the "data controller"). Contact us at {{ site_email }}, {{ site_address }}.

2. What we collect

InformationWhenWhy

Phone numberYou registerLandlords: to verify them and let tenants call or WhatsApp. Tenants: so a landlord can reach you.

We do not ask for your national ID, bank or M-Pesa details, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. What is public

  • A landlord's phone number, username and house listings, photos and reviews received are visible to anyone who visits the site.
  • A tenant's details are shown only to a landlord they contact. Reviews about tenants are private.
  • Photos may contain hidden location data (EXIF). Remove it from photos if you do not want your exact location shared.

4. How we use it

To run the site: create accounts, verify people, show listings, deliver inquiries, show reviews, send you codes and password-reset emails, keep the site secure, and answer your questions. We do not sell your information.

5. Who else handles it

We use trusted providers to run the service. They process data on our behalf:

  • Africa's Talking sends SMS codes to landlords.
  • Our email provider sends verification and password-reset emails.
  • Neon stores the database.
  • Supabase stores uploaded photos.
  • Vercel hosts the website.

Some of these providers are outside Kenya, so your data may be processed abroad. We take reasonable steps to keep it protected, and we may also share information when the law requires it, for example to a court or the police.

6. How long we keep it

  • Unfinished sign-ups are deleted after about 30 minutes.
  • Account information, listings, inquiries and reviews are kept while your account is open.
  • When you close your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except what we must keep by law or to settle a dispute.

7. How we protect it

Passwords and codes are stored hashed, forms are protected against forgery, the site uses HTTPS, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the ODPC as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • see the data we hold about you;
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to or limit certain uses;
  • receive a copy of your data in a usable format.

To use any of these, email {{ site_email }} from the address on your account. We will reply within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

9. Children

Saka Keja is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For significant changes we will also tell registered users by email.

11. Contact

Questions: {{ site_email }}. See also our Terms & Conditions.
