Careers

We are a small, new team helping Murang'a find homes. We have no advertised positions at the moment, but we are always glad to hear from people who want to help.

Where we may need help

  • Area agents. People who know a neighbourhood well and can help landlords list houses and take good photos.
  • Photographers. Clear, honest photos of houses and rooms.
  • Customer support. Answering tenants and landlords by phone, WhatsApp and email.
  • Developers and designers. Django, mobile-friendly design and testing.
How to get in touch

Email {{ site_email }} with a short note about you, what you can do, and the area you know best. We keep what you send private and reply when a suitable role opens.

Internships and volunteering will be announced here when they are available.
