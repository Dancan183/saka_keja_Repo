How to post your house

Writing the title

A clear title helps tenants find your house and call you sooner. Build it in three steps:

  1. Describe the house Start with its size.
    • A 1 bedroom
    • A spacious 1 bedroom
  2. Add what makes it special Mention one unique feature.
    • A 1 bedroom with spacious parking
    • A 2 bedroom with security cameras
  3. Add a landmark so people can find it Name something well known nearby.
    • A 1 bedroom with spacious parking near Shell Petrol Station
    • A 1 bedroom with spacious parking, Kangema Road opposite Depot
    • A 1 bedroom with spacious parking opposite Mumbi Parish
Put together A spacious 1 bedroom with parking near Shell Petrol Station, Mukuyu
